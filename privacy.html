Rainproof NYC Flood Map

What happens to what you share

Last updated Aug 14, 2026. Rebuild by Design.

This map collects flooding stories from people who live in New York City. It is run by Rebuild by Design. This page says, in plain words, what happens to a story, a pin, a photo and a contact detail once you send them.

The short version. Your story, the pin you place and any photos, video or audio you add are published on a public map after our team reads them, and Rebuild by Design may also use them on its website. Your name, email, phone and social handle are not published. Your pin is rounded to about a block, so it never points at an address. You can ask us to change or remove your story at any time and we will.

What is published

  • What you wrote about the flooding.
  • The pin, rounded to about a block. See below.
  • The neighbourhood you named.
  • Any photos, video, audio or documents you added. These may also appear on the Rebuild by Design website, which is what the tick box on the form asks you to agree to.
  • A name, but only the amount you chose. You can appear with your full name, with your first name and an initial, or with no name at all. You can also send a story to the organizing team without it ever going on the map.
  • A public contact, but only if you filled in that box yourself. It is a separate box, it is optional, and it says on the form that anyone who sees your story can see it.

What is never published

  • Your first and last name, unless you chose to appear under it.
  • Your email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your social handle.
  • The exact spot your phone reported when you tapped "use my location".

Those details are collected so the Rainproof team can reach you about your story: to check a detail, to ask before quoting you, or to tell you it is live. They are kept in a separate table from the story itself, and only people who have been given a moderator account can open it.

Your pin, and why it is not exact

If you tap "use my location" your phone gives us a precise position. At a flooded home that position is your address. So before anything is saved, we round it to three decimal places. That puts your pin somewhere inside a cell about the size of a city block, roughly 110 metres one way and 85 the other.

We do not keep the exact position anywhere. It is rounded in your browser before it is sent, and rounded again by the database when it arrives. Nothing we hold can recover the original.

The map draws stories at neighbourhood zoom, so the rounding does not change anything you can see.

Photos

A photo taken on a phone usually carries hidden information, including the GPS coordinates of where it was taken. Every photo is re-encoded in your browser before it is uploaded, which removes all of it. What reaches us is the picture and nothing else.

These are ordinary commercial services and none of them are run by Rebuild by Design or by New York University. Only named Rainproof staff hold moderator accounts, and only those accounts can read a submission before it is published or read contact details at all.

How long it is kept

  • Stories that are not published, because they were rejected or withdrawn, are deleted after 90 days, along with everything attached to them.
  • Contact details for a story that was not published are deleted on the same 90-day clock.
  • Contact details for a published story are kept while the story is on the map, so that we can reach you if you ask us to change or remove it. They go when the story goes.
  • Published stories stay on the map until you ask us to take yours down.

The deletion runs on a schedule, not by hand, so it happens whether or not anybody remembers. Stories and contact details go first, then their photos within a day.

Changing or removing your story

Write to us and say which story is yours. The reference number on your confirmation screen is the fastest way to find it, but your email address or a description works too.

We will correct it or take it down. Taking it down removes the story, the pin, the photos and the contact details, and it removes the record of any edit a moderator made to it. Nothing is kept back.

Corrections and removals: info@rebuildbydesign.org

Editing by moderators

A moderator can fix a typo or tidy the language in a story before it goes up. When that happens, the version you sent is kept alongside it and the change is logged with who made it and when. Moderators cannot delete that record. If you think an edit changed your meaning, write to us.

Children

This map is for adults. Please do not submit a story on behalf of anyone under 18, and please do not name a child in a story. If we find one we remove it.
